What you get

Everything a season needs

Members & households

One record per person, linked into households. Siblings, payers, licence numbers, medical consent and who is allowed to answer for whom.

Attendance that closes

Ask once. Families answer in a tap, replies close on a deadline, and the coach sees the count on the bench — online or not.

Line-ups & selection

Build units from who actually said yes, publish to the selected players only, and keep the sheet with the game record.

Dues without chasing

Fee plans, instalments and sibling discounts. Invoices go out per household by mail, reminders escalate on a ladder you set, and the treasurer marks off payments as they land.

News & public site

Write once, choose the audience: one team, the whole club, or the public website. Coaches can post to their own team from the app.

Your colours, your crest

Upload the logo, set two colours, done — app, website and invoices follow. Type and layout stay fixed so every screen stays readable.

Season calendar

Plan recurring practices across your ice slots, see conflicts before you generate 32 events, publish to apps and site together.

Sign-up intake

New members apply, you see what is missing at a glance, and one click approves, places them in a team and issues the invoice.

Roles & rights

A coach sees their team. A treasurer sees money. A secretary sees everyone. Nobody has to be handed the whole database.

Coming next

Club shop, season renewal in the app, and federation licence sync. On the roadmap, not in the price.

Ask about a site
What counts as a member?

Anyone on a roster this season. Staff, board and parents are free, and a player in two teams counts once — so the 250 line is further away than it looks.

Moving in mid-season?

We pro-rate to the months left, and we import your member list and calendar for you.

Any extra fees?

None beyond VAT. Members pay the club by bank transfer as they always have — RosterChief issues the invoices and tracks what is still open.

Where is our data?

In the EU, exportable at any time, and yours. Leaving takes one click and a CSV.
