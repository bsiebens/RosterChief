Signing up in the app only records interest (accept_referee_signup); a referee row exists only once staff confirmed (or assigned) someone. The fee is entered right here, inline, rather than behind a "Fee" button that opened a second (and on the dashboard, nested-inside-a-dialog) modal -- that extra click-through was the easiest way for a fee to slip past everyone's attention until PDF export time. Travel (km/rate) stays behind a small disclosure since most assignments never need it, but posts in the same form as the fee so there's still only one Save per referee. "No fee" (EventReferee.no_fee) marks someone deliberately unpaid: it hides the rest of the fee line and stops them counting as "Fee not set". The amount due with travel goes on its own line under the buttons, keeping the buttons together. {% endcomment %}
